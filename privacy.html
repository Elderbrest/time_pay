TimePay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

TimePay (“the app”) is a work-hours and earnings tracker operated by Eldar Lazhuyeu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices. By using TimePay you agree to this policy.

1. Who controls your data

The data controller is Eldar Lazhuyeu. For any privacy question or request, contact us at elderblr2@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work for you:

  • Account information — your email address and password (handled by Firebase Authentication), and your name. Your password is managed by Firebase and is never visible to us.
  • Profile details — your first and last name, and an optional profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Your work data — the working days, hours, start/end times, and notes you enter, plus your hourly rate, so the app can calculate your earnings. Earnings amounts are calculated on the fly and are not stored.
  • Analytics & diagnostics — anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, when hours are logged) via Google Analytics for Firebase, and crash/error reports via Firebase Crashlytics. These help us understand usage and fix problems. They do not include the content of your work entries or your password.

TimePay does not collect your location, and it contains no ads.

3. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To store and display your logged hours, plans, notes, and calculated earnings.
  • To generate the monthly PDF report you choose to export.
  • To understand how the app is used and to improve stability and features.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage). It is private to your account: our security rules ensure that only you, signed in, can read or write your own data. Data is encrypted in transit. Google processes this data on our behalf — see Google’s Firebase Privacy and Security information.

5. Analytics & crash reporting

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may collect an analytics identifier, device and app information, and diagnostic data such as crash logs, in line with Google’s policies. This information is used only to operate and improve TimePay. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your profile, rate, and work entries directly in the app.
  • Deletion — you can delete individual work entries in the app. To request deletion of your entire account and associated data, email us at elderblr2@gmail.com and we will remove it within a reasonable time.
  • Photo — you can remove your profile photo at any time in Settings.
  • Opt-out — you can stop all collection by signing out and uninstalling the app.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as you use TimePay and for a reasonable period afterward. On an account-deletion request, we remove your stored data.

8. Children

TimePay is intended for adults who work and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact elderblr2@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: elderblr2@gmail.com
